Quality

From raw material to canister

BluVera AUS32 is a Diesel Exhaust Fluid (DEF): a high-purity solution of 32.5% urea in demineralised water, made to ISO 22241. Injected into the exhaust of modern SCR diesel engines, it turns harmful nitrogen oxides (NOₓ) into harmless nitrogen and water. Every litre of BluVera AUS32 we distribute passes through the same four-stage control process.

01

BASF raw material

Industrial-grade urea at 32.5% purity, sourced directly from BASF.

02

Laboratory testing

pH, density and chemical stability verified for every batch.

03
